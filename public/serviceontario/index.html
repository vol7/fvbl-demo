We are here to help

Complete your services online, or find a location near you and book an appointment.

Popular services

  • Get a Used Vehicle Information Package (UVIP) 

    Selling or buying a used vehicle? Pre-approve the package online so it is ready when you visit a ServiceOntario centre.

  • Renew a driver’s licence 

    Renew online or plan your visit to a ServiceOntario centre.

  • Renew a health card 

    Ensure access to health services by renewing your health card.

  • Get renewal reminders 

    Sign up for reminders by email, text message or phone call when it’s time to renew.

  • Change your address 

    Update your address with the Government of Ontario if you’ve moved.

  • Register a birth 

    Use our 5-in-1 birth bundle to register your newborn, order a birth certificate and apply for additional benefits.

Other government services

Some services are not delivered by ServiceOntario. They have a separate process to complete. Here are a few common examples:

  • Passport, Social Insurance Number, Employment Insurance

    Visit the Government of Canada for more information.

  • Get a new driver’s licence or book a road test

    Learn how to qualify for your driver’s licence in Ontario, including booking road tests through DriveTest.

  • Exchange an out-of-province driver’s licence

    Find out how to switch a driver’s licence from another province, state or country for an Ontario driver’s licence through DriveTest.

  • OSAP (Ontario Student Assistance Program)

    Get help paying for school when you qualify for OSAP.

  • Ontario Fishing & Hunting Licences

    Get a fishing or hunting licence, enter draws and submit hunter reports through Fish and Wildlife.
